Delete your Kinzo account

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

Delete in the app

1. Open the Kinzo app and log in.

2. Tap your profile button (top right of Home).

3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.

4. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Can’t open the app?

Email contact.kinzocoin@gmail.com from the email address of your Kinzo account, with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. We will delete the account as soon as possible, and at the latest within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, username, name, date of birth, country, KINZO balance, game balances, tasks, ads, referrals and transfer history.

What is kept

A scrambled (hashed) ID of your device, so the device cannot be used for a new account. Transfers you made stay in the other user’s history without your name. Totals that do not identify you.
