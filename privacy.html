Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Kinzo (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the Kinzo app and related pages (the “Service”), why we collect it, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. Data we collect

Account data: your email address, your username and, if you sign in with Google, the basic account details Google shares with us (your email address and Google account ID).

Profile data: your first name, last name, date of birth and country, when you complete your profile.

Device data: a scrambled (hashed) version of an ID from your device, used only to allow one account per device. We do not store the original device ID.

Activity data: the tasks you complete, the rewarded ads you watched in earlier versions of the app (time and reward), referral codes you use and who used yours, KINZO you send and receive, game coin conversions, your KINZO balance, and the days on which you open the app.

Support data: messages you send us and any notes or changes our team makes to your account when helping you or checking for abuse.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.

3. Why we use it

To create and run your account and log you in; to check the age rule, the one-account-per-device rule and the other rules in our Terms; to record your KINZO balance, rewards, transfers and conversions; to stop cheating and abuse; to answer your messages; and to count simple totals such as daily users, new sign-ups and users per country, so we can improve the Service.

We process this data because it is needed to provide the Service you asked for, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the Service fair and secure, and, where the law requires it, with your consent (for example for personalised ads).

4. Ads

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. When an ad is loaded, Google may collect data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and account ID with us. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with our cloud database provider on servers in Japan (Tokyo region). If you live outside Japan, your data is transferred there. We take steps to protect it as described in this policy.

7. Who can see your data

Other users can see your username, for example when you send them KINZO or use their referral code, and the username and join date of people who used your code. They do not see your email, name, date of birth or country.

Our team can see account data when needed to run the Service, help you or check for abuse.

We share data only with service providers who help us run the Service (our cloud database provider, Google for sign-in and ads, and the app stores), when the law requires it, or to protect our users and the Service. We do not sell your personal data.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account data, profile data, balances and activity data are deleted.

We keep: the hashed device ID, so the device cannot be used for a new account; transfer records in the other user’s history, with your link to them removed; logs of team actions, with your link to them removed; and totals that do not identify you.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your login session is stored encrypted on your device. Access to the database is limited by strict rules so each user can only read their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Your choices and rights

You can see your profile and history in the app. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account, or by emailing us from your account’s email address. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, email contact.kinzocoin@gmail.com.

11. Age

The Service is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that a user is under 18, we close the account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact.kinzocoin@gmail.com.
