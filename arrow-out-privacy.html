Arrow Out – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data Arrow Out (“we”, “us”) collects when you play the Arrow Out game, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Arrow Out is a Kinzo game: you can play without an account, or log in with your Kinzo account.

2. Data kept only on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, hints and energy) and your settings (look, sound and vibration) are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us. They are removed when you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game.

3. If you log in with Kinzo (optional)

Logging in is optional. The game uses your existing Kinzo account; it does not create accounts.

When you log in, you use your Kinzo email address and password, or Google sign-in, which shares your email address and Google account ID. The game shows your Kinzo username in Settings.

While you are logged in, the game sends us the number of each new level you finish and the order of your taps in it, so our server can check the level was really played. We use this to count your levels and to give you game coins, which you can convert into KINZO in the Kinzo app. We store your counted levels, your game coin balance and the times of these reports. Watching ads never gives coins.

The Kinzo Terms of Use and Privacy Policy also apply to your Kinzo account. Kinzo accounts are only for people aged 18 or older.

4. Ads

Ads in the game are shown by Google AdMob. When an ad is loaded, Google may collect data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Where your data is stored

Data from a Kinzo login is stored with Kinzo’s cloud database provider on servers in Japan (Tokyo region). If you live outside Japan, your data is transferred there.

6. Who can see your data

We share data only with service providers who help us run the game (Kinzo’s cloud database provider, Google for sign-in and ads, and Google Play), when the law requires it, or to protect our players and the game. We do not sell your personal data.

7. How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game. Data from a Kinzo login is kept while your Kinzo account exists and is deleted when you delete your Kinzo account.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your login is saved in the game’s private storage on your phone; log out in Settings to remove it. Access to the database is limited by strict rules so each player can only read their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Your choices and rights

You can play without logging in. You can log out at any time in Settings. You can delete your Kinzo account, together with its game data, in the Kinzo app (Profile → Delete account), or from the game: Settings → Delete Kinzo account opens the deletion page. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, email contact.kinzocoin@gmail.com.

10. Children

Arrow Out is not directed at children under 13, and the Kinzo login and game coins are only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact.kinzocoin@gmail.com.
